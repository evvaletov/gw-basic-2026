LANGUAGE WHICH HAS THE SAME OR VERY NEARLY THE SAME MEANING.
I CHOOSE A WORD -- YOU TYPE A SYNONYM.
IF YOU CAN'T THINK OF A SYNONYM, TYPE THE WORD 'HELP'
AND I WILL TELL YOU A SYNONYM.
